Welcome to La Petite Lavande 🌿💜

A taste of Provence in every bite. Tucked away in a quiet corner of the city, La Petite Lavande brings the charm of a rustic French café to your table. Inspired by the rolling lavender fields of Provence, our menu is crafted with fresh ingredients, delicate flavors, and a touch of Parisian elegance. Start your morning with a buttery croissant and a rich café au lait, or indulge in our signature lavender-infused pastries. Whether you're here for a leisurely brunch or a cozy afternoon tea, we invite you to slow down, savor the moment, and enjoy the simple pleasures of French cuisine.

Our Menu

Drinks

Café au Lait – Classic French-style coffee with equal parts rich coffee and steamed milk.

About Us 🌿💜

La Petite Lavande was born from a love of French café culture and the tranquil beauty of the lavender fields of Provence. Our café is a haven where the simple pleasures of life are celebrated—where you can savor every bite, sip, and moment in a warm, welcoming atmosphere. Founded with the vision to bring a taste of the French countryside to the heart of the city, La Petite Lavande offers a menu inspired by fresh, seasonal ingredients and the delicate flavors of Provence. From the first whiff of freshly baked croissants to the last sip of a rich café au lait, we want every visit to feel like a little escape to a quaint Parisian bistro. At La Petite Lavande, we believe that food should be both a joy and an experience. That’s why our dishes feature the delicate essence of lavender—an ingredient we hold dear, sourced locally and used to elevate our pastries, teas, and more. Our café is more than just a place to eat; it's a space to unwind, to connect with friends, or to enjoy a quiet moment alone with a book and a cup of tea. With soft lavender hues, rustic French décor, and a menu that celebrates the art of French cooking, we invite you to experience the charm and warmth of Provence, right here in the city.

Bon appétit!

Meet the Owner 🌸

Sophie Dubois grew up in the French countryside, inspired by the beauty of lavender fields. After years of working in Parisian cafés, she opened La Petite Lavande to bring the charm of Provence to the city. With a love for French cuisine and hospitality, Sophie aims to create a warm, welcoming space where guests can relax and savor delicious, lavender-infused dishes.
